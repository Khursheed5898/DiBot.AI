# Project Vision: DiBot.Ai 🌟

DiBot.Ai is a transparent AI debate partner designed to sharpen critical thinking skills through intelligent debate practice. Built specifically for education, it allows users to see the AI's internal reasoning process, helping them learn to construct and deconstruct logic more effectively.

## Core Objectives
- **Transparency**: Reveal the "black box" of AI logic.
- **Education**: Train users in structured debate and critical thinking.
- **Engagement**: Provide a premium, high-fidelity experience with real-time feedback.

## Target Audience
- Students and educators.
- Aspiring debaters and public speakers.
- Anyone looking to improve their logical reasoning.
